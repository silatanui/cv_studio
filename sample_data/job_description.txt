Job Title: Lead Backend Platform Engineer
Company: Apex Global Technologies
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid / Remote)

About the Role:
Apex Global Technologies is seeking an exceptional Lead Backend Platform Engineer to spearhead our next-generation distributed cloud services and infrastructure platform. You will lead the architectural design, implementation, and optimization of low-latency backend systems that power millions of enterprise transactions worldwide.

Key Responsibilities:
- Architect, build, and maintain mission-critical distributed microservices and APIs using Python and modern asynchronous frameworks.
- Lead latency optimization, database tuning, and caching strategies across PostgreSQL, Redis, and cloud-native datastores.
- Drive cloud infrastructure automation and container orchestration on AWS using Kubernetes and Terraform.
- Champion CI/CD automation, unit testing best practices, and technical governance across engineering squads.
- Mentor junior and mid-level software engineers through code reviews and architecture deep dives.

Requirements & Qualifications:
- 5+ years of software engineering experience with deep expertise in Python, FastAPI/Django, and distributed systems.
- Strong hands-on experience with AWS cloud services (EC2, RDS, Lambda, ECS/EKS) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Proven mastery in relational database optimization (PostgreSQL) and in-memory caches (Redis).
- Experience designing and scaling high-throughput event-driven or microservices architectures.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
- Outstanding communication and cross-functional leadership capabilities.

Keywords / Priority Stack:
Distributed Systems, Python, FastAPI, AWS, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Redis, Latency Optimization, Microservices, CI/CD
