Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about optimizing your CV with CV Studio.

Is CV Studio free, and what is its purpose?
Yes! CV Studio is completely free to use. It is a personal engineering portfolio project created by software engineer Tanui Sila Kipngetich to demonstrate advanced AI resume tailoring, semantic ATS matching, and full-stack software development. It is strictly non-commercial: there are no fees, paywalls, trial limits, or watermarks on generated documents.
How does the Zero-Hallucination guarantee work?
Unlike general-purpose chatbots that invent qualifications, our multi-stage pipeline strictly cross-references every generated bullet against your original resume. If an achievement or skill wasn't in your original background, our system flags it and prevents it from appearing on your tailored CV.
Will my resume pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)?
All our templates are built according to strict ATS standards. They use clear semantic headings, standard font hierarchies, and zero unreadable text frames or graphics that cause ATS parsers like Workday, Taleo, and Greenhouse to fail.
Can I edit the generated resume before downloading?
Absolutely. Our built-in WYSIWYG editor allows you to edit text inline, add or remove sections, reorder bullet points, change templates, fonts, and accent colors in real time before exporting to Word or PDF.
How is my personal data protected?
We prioritize privacy above all. You do not need to register an account. Your resume content is never sold or used to train public machine learning models. You can purge all session data at any time with one click.
